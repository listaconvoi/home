Con Voi

An independent, community-led group of candidates, formed to continue and expand our work leading Manchester Comites in recent years. We want every Italian community across the North of England to have a voice on Comites, bringing together the expertise and experience of MIE and Italia4Italy.

During our time leading Manchester Comites, we have turned the community's concerns into tangible results:

  • A dedicated task force to support the Consulate, tackling delays with passports, identity cards and AIRE applications: a campaign that took us all the way to a hearing at the Italian Chamber of Deputies on the state of consular services.
  • The "Gocce d'Italia" project, an 18-part documentary series telling the stories of more than 130 Italians in the United Kingdom.
  • Free information webinars on the EU Settlement Scheme, travel rules and Italian tax identification numbers, helping people navigate procedures, rights and services in the UK.
  • Consular representation on the Isle of Man, with the process of appointing a consular correspondent launched in August.
  • Visits and meetings across the consular district to listen to Italian residents and understand their needs.

    Our programme

    Here is how we plan to continue representing, supporting and strengthening the Italian community in the North of England.

    A strong voice for Italians in the North of England

    We will continue to raise our community's concerns consistently, not only with the Manchester Consulate, but also with the Embassy, the General Council of Italians Abroad (CGIE), the Ministry, the Government and Parliament. Through ongoing scrutiny, cooperation and advocacy, we will work to improve access to essential services and their quality for Italian citizens and businesses living and operating in the North of England.

    • More resources and better consular services. We will continue to use every tool available to Comites to press for action on waiting times for passports, identity cards, AIRE and civil registration, and on the difficulties people face when trying to contact consular offices, just as we successfully did with the task force. We will keep pressing for the resources the Consulate needs to offer more appointments and end the deeply frustrating Prenot@mi "lottery".
    • Simpler procedures and clearer information. We will set up a working group to help the Consulate produce simpler forms, easy-to-follow instructions, clear checklists for each service and consistent guidance on the documents required, processing times and procedures, with translations into several languages.
    • Modernise and digitise consular procedures. We will work with the Consulate and the Ministry to identify which paper-based or postal procedures could be simplified or moved online, starting with passport renewals, photo authentication for children, issuing certificates, and population register and AIRE applications.
    • No one should be left behind. We will pay particular attention to older people, disabled people and those facing difficulties, negotiating easier access to consular services and free assistance at the Comites office so that the move to digital services does not shut anyone out of essential support.

    A Comites that is close to people and active across the district

    We want Comites to be a reliable place to turn to for guidance, to raise concerns affecting the community and to get help accessing services.

    • A Comites office in Manchester that is open to the public. We want to open an accessible office near the Consulate, with regular opening hours. We intend to employ administrative staff to provide information, record concerns and offer free help and support to Italian citizens.
    • Continue and strengthen the Citizens' Advice Desk. We will continue to collect reports of difficulties to identify recurring problems, and keep a dedicated opportunity for discussion with the Consulate at every Comites assembly meeting.
    • Bring consular services closer to communities. We will continue actively supporting and pressing the requests we have already put forward through Comites for more permanent consular offices across the district, particularly in Leeds. We will also continue working with the Consulate, as we did in 2026, to bring staff and services to areas further from Manchester, expanding the range of services beyond passports wherever possible.
    • Understand the scale of problems and ensure greater transparency. We will promote qualitative and quantitative research into the main challenges facing the community and access to services, to develop solutions grounded in sound evidence and data. We will publish the requests made by Comites, the responses received, the projects launched, the resources used and the results achieved.

    Keep the connection with Italy alive and open it up to younger generations

    Language and culture are among the strongest ties between Italian communities abroad and Italy, particularly for younger generations.

    • Support Italian language and culture courses. We will work with local associations and education providers to promote and strengthen the courses available to children, young people and adults.
    • Help second and third generations connect with the Italian language. We will support initiatives that help the children and grandchildren of Italian migrants maintain or rediscover their linguistic and cultural connection with Italy, encouraging them to study Italian at GCSE and A-level too.
    • Promote Italian culture across the district. We will organise and support events and activities celebrating film, literature, music, theatre, art, science and traditions, working with associations, universities, schools and cultural institutions.
    • Connect Italian and British organisations. We will encourage cultural and educational partnerships, exchanges and twinning projects that build lasting relationships and reach new audiences.

    Inform, guide and connect the Italian community

    The Italian community in the North of England includes people who arrived just months ago and families who have been here for generations: workers, students, professionals, business owners, pensioners and citizens with very different migration experiences. Comites must represent them all.

    • Clear, timely information about life in the UK. We will continue producing articles, guides, meetings and webinars on residence, employment, welfare, healthcare, schools, public services and changes to rules and regulations affecting Italians.
    • Continue the "New Italians" project. We will strengthen this project for Italian citizens who have moved to the UK after previously migrating to Italy, helping them access information, take part in civic life and build more direct relationships with Italian institutions.
    • A Comites with a genuine presence across the district. We will support and draw on local associations, professionals, businesses and communities, making our councillors in different towns and cities a local point of contact and a link with Manchester, while building a wider network of local Comites representatives.

    Preserve and share the history of every Italian community across the district

    The history of Italian migration to the North of England belongs both to today's community and to generations to come.

    • Continue our film and documentary projects. We will build on the "Drops of Italy" documentary series, developing new projects to record first-hand accounts from Italians living across the district.
    • Collect and preserve the community's memories. We want to preserve and share personal accounts, photographs, documents and memories of Italian migration, creating a collection that future generations can access too.
    • Involve young people, schools and future generations. We will help bring the history of Italian migration to people who have not experienced it themselves, developing short courses and teaching resources for Italian schools about the history of Italians in the UK.
    • Tell the stories of every community in the district. We will celebrate the different local histories of Italian migration, from Manchester to Yorkshire, from Lancashire to Merseyside, and from the northern Midlands to the Isle of Man.

    In recent years, the partnership between MIE and Italia4Italy has helped Comites move beyond years of inaction and start delivering tangible results. It secured a task force to strengthen consular services, brought the community's concerns to national institutions, secured consular representation on the Isle of Man, supported consular visits across the district, and promoted cultural, information and heritage projects. Help us continue this work: vote "Con Voi"!

    How to vote

    Comites elections are held by post. You must be registered on the electoral roll to vote.

    1. 1

      Check that you are eligible

      You can vote if you are an Italian citizen aged 18 or over and have been registered with AIRE in the Manchester consular district (see the area covered) since at least 4 June 2026. Contact us with any questions!

    2. 2

      Register to vote using SPID or CIE

      With a digital identity (SPID or CIE), you can register without printing anything. Click here, sign in with SPID or CIE, go to the population register section and select the option to register on the electoral roll for Comites elections. Follow the steps: tick the boxes, generate the form and submit it to the Consulate. You must do this by 4 November: do not leave it until the last minute!

      Go to Fast-IT
    3. 2b

      Register to vote using a paper form

      You can still register without a digital identity. Fill in the electoral registration form and sign it by hand. Take a photo or scan of the form and email it to consmanchester.elettorale@esteri.it, attaching a copy of an identity document that shows your signature. You must do this by 4 November: do not leave it until the last minute!

      Download the form
    4. 3

      Receive your voting pack

      Once your registration has been checked, your voting pack, including the ballot paper, will be sent in November to the home address you provided on the form.

    5. 4

      Vote and post your ballot paper

      Put a cross on the "Con Voi" list symbol. You may also choose up to six candidates from our list by putting a cross beside each of their names. Return your ballot paper by post in the prepaid envelope provided, following the deadlines set out in your voting pack.

    For the official voting procedures and the latest deadlines, visit the Italian Consulate in Manchester's website:

    What is Comites?

    Comites is the Committee of Italians Abroad, directly elected by Italian citizens living in the consular district to represent their community in its dealings with the Consulate and Italian institutions. Its members are unpaid volunteers. It gives Italians living here a collective voice, bringing their concerns, needs and proposals to the institutions that can address them.

    On the current Manchester Comites website you can read about its work in recent years: improving consular services, keeping the community informed, promoting Italian language and culture, and helping Italian citizens. We want to continue and expand this work: find out more.

    Representation

    Brings the Italian community's needs to the Consulate and other institutions, puts forward proposals, offers opinions and raises issues affecting citizens living in the district.

    Rights and information

    Works with the Consulate to protect the rights and interests of Italians abroad and helps share useful information about the services and opportunities available.

    Community building

    Works with associations, organisations and local groups, promoting initiatives that support integration, young people, welfare, schools, training and the community's social life.

    Language and culture

    Helps maintain the connection with Italy and promotes Italian language, culture, history and migration heritage, involving younger generations too.

    Manchester Comites

    The Manchester Comites represents Italians living in the Manchester consular district, which covers much of northern and central England.

    With more than 125,000 Italian citizens living in the district, the 2026 elections will choose 18 councillors. For the next five years, they will represent this community in its dealings with the Consulate.
